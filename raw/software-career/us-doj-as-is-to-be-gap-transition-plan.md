# DOJ IT Strategic Plan: As-Is, To-Be, Gap Analysis, and Transition Plan Definitions

> Source: https://www.justice.gov/archive/jmd/irm/imss/2002itplan/appendix_f.htm
> Collected: 2026-09-21
> Published: Unknown

Current Architecture is the current state of an enterprise's architecture. The “As Is” model is the representation of the cumulative “as-built” or baseline of the existing architecture.

Gap Analysis is the process whereby an enterprise identifies, and determines the effort required to correct gaps or deficiencies between its desired target architecture and its actual current architecture.

Target Architecture is the target state of an enterprise's architecture. The “To Be” model is the representation of a desired future state or “to-be-built” within the context of the strategic direction.

Transition Plan is the blueprint or plan that helps an enterprise bridge the gap between its “As Is” and “To Be” models. The plan should identify required transition activities, priorities, and milestones or timelines.
