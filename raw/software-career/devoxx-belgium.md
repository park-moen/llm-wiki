## Devoxx Belgium

Devoxx Belgium 2027 will once again transform Antwerp into the meeting place for developers, architects, builders, and innovators shaping the future of software engineering.

One thing is becoming increasingly clear: we are entering a new era where developers are evolving into builders. AI Agents, autonomous workflows, Spec-Driven Development, and ever more capable coding assistants are fundamentally reshaping how software is designed, built, reviewed, and maintained. From multi-agent architectures (A2A) and Model Context Protocol (MCP) to local LLM clusters, GPU inference, and next-generation Java AI libraries, Devoxx Belgium 2027 will explore a future where humans and intelligent systems collaborate side by side.

With Java 29 expected around the time of Devoxx Belgium 2027, the Java ecosystem continues to prove why it remains at the heart of modern software development. As innovation accelerates around concurrency, performance, cloud-native development, and AI integration, Java is evolving into a powerful foundation for the next generation of intelligent software systems.

Under the theme “FROM DEVELOPER TO BUILDER”, Devoxx Belgium 2027 will explore the rise of Agentic Engineering, AI-powered software creation, local and cloud-based AI ecosystems, intelligent developer tooling, and the evolving role of developers in an age where software increasingly builds software itself.

The future is not about replacing developers. It’s about giving builders superpowers.

But Devoxx has never only been about technology. It’s about community. Thousands of passionate developers, speakers, sponsors, and technology leaders will once again come together in Antwerp to exchange ideas, share knowledge, and help shape what comes next. See you in Antwerp for Devoxx Belgium 2027.

## Two kinds of days, one week

Start the week going deep on a few topics, then spend three days sampling everything the industry is working on. Want both? The [Combi pass](https://devoxx.be/tickets) covers Deep Dive and Conference days.

Three-hour sessions, hands-on labs and lunch talks. You leave with working code, not just slides.

Evenings

## About Devoxx

Devoxx, a series of technology conferences, is held in various countries including Belgium, France, Poland, Morocco, and Greece. Additionally, VoxxedDays (a Devoxx incubator) is organized in various locations globally (including CERN).

Each year, Devoxx welcomes over 20,000 passionate developers from around the world to learn, share, and connect through its events.
