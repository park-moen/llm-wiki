## Frequently Asked Questions

### Get answers to your QCon San Francisco Video-Only Pass Questions

What is the QCon Video-Only Pass?

The Video-Only Pass allows you to watch most sessions that were recorded in-person at the QCon San Francisco conference.

These sessions will be made available two weeks after the in-person event. All Video-Only Pass holders will receive an email with their login credentials once the video access has been enabled.

Viewing time for all the technical content is around 60+ hours - you will be able to watch all these sessions at your own pace. Videos will remain available for 12 months.

You can learn more about the [Video-Only Pass](https://qconsf.com/video-only) here.

What does the Video-Only Pass include?

The Video-Only Pass includes exclusive access to most sessions that were recorded at the in-person event. You will have access to these sessions by logging into your my account page.

When will session recordings be available?

The session recordings will be available starting on December 21st, 2026, and will remain available for the duration of 12 months following the conference.

All users will receive an email with their login credentials once the video access has been enabled.

Will QCon San Francisco be available as a live stream for Video-Only Pass holders?

There are no live-streamed sessions.

If I have trouble watching session recordings, what troubleshooting do you recommend?

Make sure you have a fast and reliable internet connection. The latest version of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge browser. Apple Safari is not supported. We strongly recommend choosing Google Chrome.

If you are having issues:

- Make sure your setup is compatible with the above requirements.

- Sometimes, it might take a moment before the room loads. If you are stuck on the loading screen, try refreshing the page (CTRL+F5 on Windows or Command+R on Mac).

- Check your internet speed/quality and system.

- It is important to disconnect from any VPN servers you might be using and ensure your firewall is configured properly. If connecting from a corporate network, make sure to forward the Firewall configuration to your IT department.

- Try disabling your browser’s ad/cookie blocker.

- If the problem persists, try using a different browser. If that helps, it means the original browser is causing the problem. Try clearing its cache and cookies to fix the problem.

- Try using a different internet connection (ex: mobile hotspot). If that helps, it means your original internet connection is causing the issues (potentially the VPN setup).

Useful links:

- Clearing cache and cookies (Chrome): [https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32050](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32050)

- Update Chrome: [https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95414](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95414)
