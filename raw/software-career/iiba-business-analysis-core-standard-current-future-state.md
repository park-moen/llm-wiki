## Strategy Analysis

Strategy defines the most effective way to apply the capabilities of an enterprise in order to reach a desired set of goals and objectives. Strategy Analysis describes the business analysis work performed with stakeholders to identify a strategic or tactical business need, enable the enterprise to address that need, and align the resulting change strategy with higher- and lower-level strategies.

Strategy analysis focuses on defining the future and transition states needed to address the business need. It provides context to requirements analysis and design definition for a given change. Its tasks include Analyze Current State, Define Future State, Assess Risks, and Define Change Strategy.

## Analyze Current State

The purpose of Analyze Current State is to understand the reasons why an enterprise needs to change some aspect of how it operates and what would be directly or indirectly affected by the change.

Analyze Current State understands the business need and how it relates to the way the enterprise functions today. It sets a baseline and context for change.

The Current State Description includes the context of the enterprise’s scope, capabilities, resources, performance, culture, dependencies, infrastructure, external influences, and significant relationships between these elements.

## Define Future State

The purpose of Define Future State is to determine the set of necessary conditions to meet the business need.

Define Future State defines goals and objectives that demonstrate that the business need has been satisfied and identifies what parts of the enterprise need to change in order to meet those goals and objectives.

The Future State Description includes boundaries of proposed new, removed, and modified components and the potential value expected from the future state. It may include desired capabilities, policies, resources, dependencies, infrastructure, external influences, and relationships between each element.

## Assess Risks and Define Change Strategy

Assess Risks understands uncertainties around the change, considers their effect on the ability to deliver value, and recommends actions to address risks where appropriate.

The purpose of Define Change Strategy is to develop and assess alternative approaches to the change, and then select the recommended approach. Define Change Strategy performs a gap analysis between current and future state, assesses options for achieving the future state, and recommends the highest-value approach, including any transition states required along the way.
