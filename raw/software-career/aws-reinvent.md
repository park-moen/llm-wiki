## A day at re:Invent

Five days, 2,200+ sessions, and countless opportunities to build your network. Here's what a single day looks like.

### For those who build and implement

Your day moves fast. A new service launches, and you’re immediately hands-on with it. You join a debate and solve the architecture problem you’ve been wrestling with for weeks. You sit down with the engineers who built the services you rely on and walk away with the answers you need to move forward.

LAUNCH SESSIONS

 Hands-on with new services the same day they're announced

BUILDERS’ SESSIONS

 One problem, 15 practitioners, candid discussion of what worked and what didn't

ASK THE EXPERTS

 Direct access to the experts who build, develop, and deploy these services daily— bring your hardest questions

500-LEVEL DEEP DIVES

 Go deeper than the documentation by digging into the science and cutting-edge research behind the technology

INDUSTRY MEETUPS

 Peers from different industries who solved your challenges a different way

 BETWEEN SESSIONS
Drop into the Expo, catch a live demo you didn't plan on, grab a coffee in a community hub

### For those who lead and strategize

Your day is structured around your most pressing decisions. Customer stories show you real outcomes and real tradeoffs from your industry. Working sessions let you pressure-test your strategy. You leave each day with evidence, connections, and clarity you can bring back to your team.

CUSTOMER STORIES

 Real decisions from your industry — what worked, what didn't, why they pivoted

CONNECTED LEARNING JOURNEYS

 A curated path for your role — no assembling a schedule from over 2,200 sessions

INTERACTIVE WORKING SESSIONS

 Pressure-test your cloud strategy

DEMOS

 Before-and-after business impact — concrete evidence for stakeholders

EXECUTIVE MEETUPS
From startup founders to enterprise executives, connect with peers who are navigating the same transformation decisions as you.

BETWEEN SESSIONS
A coffee, a live demo you didn't plan on, meeting with a partner who's deployed what you're evaluating
