WHAT IS DEVCON

## Three days in Brooklyn for the people putting agents to work in real codebases.

[Venue: Industry City, Building 5, Brooklyn.](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=40.6572385%2C-74.0075533&query_place_id=ChIJo2EvIVhbwokRR5XobNHAHpw)

AI DevCon brings together developers, engineering leaders, and teams using agentic coding in practice. For 2026, the focus is how teams move from individual agents to systems that build, verify and ship software they can trust.

Expect real-world AI engineering, practical dev tooling, and hands-on sessions from the people shipping with agents.

## Conference topics

Skills are the new code. They’re also the building blocks of the software factory.

[Submit a talk](https://sessionize.com/ai-native-devcon-nyc-2026/)

Rolling review. Closes 1st Oct 2026.

Context & Skills Engineering

How teams design specs, rules, and reusable skills, treating that context as code with the same rigor as the software it produces.

Agent Governance, Security & Trust

Scanning, scoring, and auditing what agents install and run: the practices that make autonomous agents safe in production.

Standardization & Reuse at Scale

Moving from solo contributors reinventing the wheel to a shared, versioned library of skills and workflows the whole org can trust.

Continuous Evaluation & Optimization

Why "installed" isn't the same as "used": measuring real activation, running evals like unit tests, and closing the loop.

Tools-in-Action: Real-World Playbooks

Honest, specific accounts of what worked and what didn't: enterprise transformation stories and live demos, not polished pitches.
