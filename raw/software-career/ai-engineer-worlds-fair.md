## AIE for Everyone

From builders to executives, startups to Fortune 500s, researchers to creatives, the AI Engineer World's Fair is where the people shaping the future of AI come together.

Learn how teams build and deploy production-ready AI systems, agents, copilots, evals, RAG pipelines, and scalable infrastructure with real-world workflows and tooling from companies shipping AI today.

Discover how enterprises are adopting AI across operations, security, procurement, customer experience, and internal workflows while managing governance and organizational change.

Gain insights into enterprise AI strategy, technical leadership, systems architecture, governance, and scaling AI adoption across organizations.

Some of the most valuable conversations happen outside the sessions. Connect with engineers, founders, researchers, and AI leaders through networking, demos, and community meetups throughout the event.

Discover how AI-native startups are finding product-market fit, scaling teams, raising capital, and building durable businesses in a rapidly evolving market. Connect with founders, operators, and investors shaping the future of AI.

Explore how AI is transforming diagnostics, drug discovery, clinical workflows, and patient care while navigating regulatory requirements and data privacy challenges.

AI in trading, risk assessment, fraud detection, compliance, and financial analysis. How quantitative finance and fintech are being transformed by foundation models.

Dive into frontier research across reasoning, multimodal systems, memory, alignment, evaluation, and next-generation model architectures. Connect with researchers advancing the future of AI capabilities.

Set the industry agenda for higher data quality - with top human data and RL env vendors paying close attention. Learn how teams source, evaluate, license, and manage high-quality datasets for training, fine-tuning, evaluation, and enterprise AI workflows.

Explore the future of human-AI interaction, AI UX, copilots, workflows, and designing intuitive AI-powered experiences for real users.

Own your AI stack. Learn about privacy, sovereignty, and running your own AI clusters from experts at NVIDIA, r/LocalLlama, and Exo. On-device inference, edge deployment, and self-hosted models for teams that need full control.
