## Explore GTC 2026 Session Replays

Talks

### CUDA: New Features and Beyond

Presented by one of the architects of NVIDIA® CUDA®, this engineering-focused talk will update you about what’s new, and what's coming next, for both CUDA and GPU computing as a whole.

[View On-Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/session-catalog/sessions/gtc26-s81859/)

Talks

### Turning Models Into Engines: The AI Factory Era

Built on open source foundations, security by design, sustainability at scale, and digital sovereignty, Mistral AI’s approach helps unlock bare-metal GPU performance, build trusted, future-ready AI systems, and advance collaboration by giving innovators access to the same infrastructure powering our models.

[View On-Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/session-catalog/sessions/gtc26-s81715/)

Talks

### How to Build End-to-End Physical AI Systems for Humanoid Robots

In this session, experts will walk through practical, end-to-end workflows for physical AI development using NVIDIA Isaac™ and Omniverse™ technologies.

[View On-Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/session-catalog/sessions/gtc26-s81478/)

Tutorial

### Agentic AI with Open Source Models: Architecture, Fine-Tuning, and Deployment Tip

We'll walk through a practical decision guide for open models from picking the right-sized models based on the agentic tasks to software for customizing, evaluating, and deploying the models at scale.

[View On-Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/session-catalog/sessions/gtc26-s81702/)

Panel

### Diffusion Unlocked: Advanced Techniques for Training, Inference, and Deployment

We’ll dive into debating the concrete architectural trade-offs required for low latency and high throughput. This includes unique training challenges, new model architectures, inference and runtime optimization, low-precision and NVFP4, and scaling topologies.

[View On-Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/session-catalog/sessions/gtc26-s81684/)

Talks

### Integrating AI and Quantum Computing to Accelerate the Future of Supercomputing

This talk outlines a vision to accelerate scientific discovery for everyone, everywhere, through the complete convergence of AI with quantum hardware and systems.

[View On-Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/session-catalog/sessions/gtc26-s81804/)

Panel

### Future of Industrial Edge

The session will highlight challenges in deploying scalable, safety-critical edge AI and reveal how these global innovators are architecting their next-generation solutions using NVIDIA’s accelerated computing and end-to-end edge platform.

[View On-Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/session-catalog/sessions/gtc26-s81844/)

Talks

### Practical Context Engineering: Eliminate Bugs With High-Signal AI Code

This session explores how you can build the right context-engineering architecture that helps LLMs deliver high-signal AI code reviews.

[View On-Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/session-catalog/sessions/gtc26-s81612/)

## Key Themes and Content From GTC 2026

### Agentic AI and Reasoning AI

Learn from industry experts how to build specialized AI agents that reason, plan, and act to deliver real business results with the latest open models, libraries, frameworks, and platforms.

[View Sessions On Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/sessions/agentic-ai/)

### AI Factories and Scaling Infrastructure

See how AI factories and next‑generation infrastructure can turn your data centers into scalable intelligence engines that efficiently produce, manage, and deploy AI across your enterprise.

[View Sessions On Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/sessions/enterprise-ai-factory/)

### AI for Science

Discover how accelerated computing and AI are transforming scientific discovery, fusing simulation, data, and machine learning to unlock breakthroughs in climate, materials, biology, and beyond.

[View Sessions On Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/sessions/ai-for-science)

### CUDA, Libraries, and Developer Tools

Dive into CUDA, CUDA-X libraries, and developer tools that let you build, optimize, and deploy GPU‑accelerated applications.

[View Sessions On Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/sessions/cuda-libraries-and-dev-tools/)

### High-Performance Inference and Training

Learn how to scale model training and AI inference together so your most demanding reasoning models, agents, and robotics workloads run faster, cheaper, and more reliably in production.

[View Sessions On Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/sessions/scaling-ai-inference-with-nvidia/)

### Open Models

Explore the new universe of open models, data, and tools that let you customize deeply, integrate quickly into your agent architectures, and deploy trustworthy AI tailored to your domain.

[View Sessions On Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/session-catalog/?keyThemes=Open%20Models)

### Physical AI and Robotics

See how NVIDIA open models, libraries, and simulation frameworks are helping developers build the next generation of factories, robots, and autonomous vehicles.

[View Sessions On Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/sessions/physical-ai-days)

### Quantum Computing and HPC

Discover how quantum computing and HPC, unified on accelerated platforms, enable hybrid workflows that push the frontiers of science, engineering, and industry at unprecedented scale.

[View Sessions On Demand](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/sessions/quantum-computing-and-hpc/)
