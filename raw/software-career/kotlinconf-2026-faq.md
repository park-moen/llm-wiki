## For all attendees

You don’t need to have actual tickets! We have you (and anyone else you registered with) listed in our records. You’ll just need to show us a QR code, either as a hard copy printout or simply displayed on your mobile device. You can also show a form of photo ID, but QR codes help us speed up the registration process. If you made your purchase using an Academia discount, remember to bring along your academic ID.

To see who’s registered under your order or to download the QR code, please click on the link included in your registration confirmation email (the subject is “Your order: #####”, and the # symbols refer to your order code).

Don’t worry! Just visit [pretix.eu/jetbrains/kotlinconf2026/resend/](https://pretix.eu/jetbrains/kotlinconf2026/resend/) and enter the email address you used to register.

If you have a voucher, enter it in the Redeem a voucher field before clicking the Buy button. Make sure you redeem your voucher before proceeding as we cannot refund orders that have been inadvertently placed without first attaching a voucher.

We can provide you with a letter of invitation for your visa application. If you need one, please send your full details along with the receipt of your KotlinConf ticket purchase to info@kotlinconf.com.

Tickets are non-refundable, except in the event that your visa is denied or the event is canceled. You can, however, transfer your ticket to another person by changing the attendee’s name.

You can modify details of the orders, such as attendee names or answers to questions. On your Order page, you will see a Change details button. Clicking on it will take you back through the checkout process, where you can change the personal details of the attendee.

Please note that you can only change order details until May 19, 2026.

We offer a bank transfer option when the minimum order is groups of 5 or more people. If this is the case, please contact us at info@kotlinconf.com.

## For speakers

Yes. If you are chosen as a speaker, you’ll be fully refunded.

You can submit up to three talk proposals.

We provide speakers of the regular talks (duration of 45 minutes) with travel and accommodation. If you have a co-speaker, travel and accommodation will only be covered for one of you. Travel and accommodation expenses are covered ONLY for full-length sessions, not lightning talks.

You can only have one co-speaker.

Unfortunately not. Once a talk has been accepted, you cannot add a co-speaker.

We provide speakers with a conference pass, which only provides access on conference days. Workshop speakers can participate in the conference as well.

As a speaker, you’re entitled to a 20% discount on one additional conference ticket. This discount doesn’t apply to workshop tickets.

We’ll confirm all accepted talks by February 10, 2026.

We don’t have a KotlinConf 2026 presentation template. Feel free to use your own. The only two slides we ask you to add to your slide deck are an initial title slide and a penultimate or final voting slide to remind the audience to evaluate your session.

For a Platinum level partner there is an opportunity to sponsor one speaker. For the success of the overall programme, the programme committee reserves the right to advise what the subject of the talk should be.

If you have any questions, please contact us at speakers@kotlinconf.com
